Our Advocacy

In support of BiyaHEROES, we advocate for accessible and comfortable seating for commuters, especially those in underserved and rural communities who spend long hours waiting for public transportation.

Through fashion, we aim to raise awareness and support initiatives that make everyday commuting more comfortable and inclusive.

About Us

PASADA Clothing is a socially-driven clothing brand built on the belief that fashion can create meaningful change. In support of BiyaHEROES, we aim to improve the daily commuting experience by advocating for accessible and comfortable seating for commuters, especially in underserved and rural communities.

We bring together quality apparel and social advocacy to raise awareness of the challenges commuters face every day. Through every design and initiative, we hope to inspire people to support a more comfortable, inclusive, and commuter-friendly future.
